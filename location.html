Getting here is easy on foot if you are anywhere near campus. We are on the Ave — the stretch of University Way that runs through the middle of the U-District — so most of the neighborhood is a few minutes away. There is no shortage of restaurants near UW, and we are the one doing phở and bánh mì to order rather than holding it under a lamp.

If you are hunting for food near the University of Washington between classes, the point of this place is that you do not wait for it. Most orders go in on a phone before anyone walks through the door. Put it through Square and pick it up when it is ready. If you want to read what is in everything first, the full menu of phở, bánh mì and rice bowls has every description and price.

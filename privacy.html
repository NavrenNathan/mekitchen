Privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

This page explains what information Mê Kitchen’s website collects, why, and who else is involved in running it. The short version: we get what you choose to send us through a form, plus statistics about how the site is used, and we don’t sell any of it.

Who we are

Mê Kitchen is a Vietnamese restaurant at 4213 University Wy NE, Seattle, WA 98105. For anything on this page, email us at mekitchen.wa@gmail.com or call 206-656-8878.

What we collect

Only what you type into one of our forms:

  • Contact form (home page): your name, email address and message.
  • Job applications (careers page): your name, email address, phone number, the position you want, your availability, anything else you choose to tell us, and a resume or cover letter if you attach one.
  • Opening news sign-up (our coming-soon page): your email address.

Browsing the site doesn’t ask you for anything. Like any website, our web host receives standard technical details when your browser loads a page, such as your IP address and browser type.

We also use Google Analytics to see how the site is used: which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are (to the nearest city), the kind of device and browser, and which buttons are tapped, such as Order online, Call or Directions.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message.
  • To review your job application and get in touch about it.
  • To see how the site is used, so we can make it better.
  • To email you news about Mê Kitchen if you signed up for it. Every email will tell you how to stop getting them, and we’ll take you off the list when you ask.

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t use it for advertising.

Who else is involved

A few outside services help run the site. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Netlify hosts the website and receives and stores what you send through our forms, including any files you attach.
  • Our email provider delivers form submissions to our inbox.
  • Google Analytics measures how the site is used. We’ve turned off its advertising features, so it isn’t used to show you ads.
  • Square runs our online ordering and loyalty program. When you order or join, you’re on Square’s site and its privacy policy applies.

Our pages also link to Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Google Maps and Apple Maps. Those sites are covered by their own policies once you open them.

Cookies and tracking

Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser so it can tell one visit from another. They aren’t used for advertising, and we don’t use any other trackers. To opt out, block cookies in your browser settings or install Google’s opt-out add-on.

The site also keeps a small setting or two in your browser for the length of your visit, such as remembering that the opening animation has already played; they’re removed when you close the tab.

The site doesn’t change how it works in response to “Do Not Track” browser signals; use the options above instead.

How long we keep it

We keep form submissions only as long as we need them: messages until we’ve dealt with them, applications while we’re hiring for the role, and sign-up emails until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in our Google Analytics account. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

Your choices

Email mekitchen.wa@gmail.com to see, correct or delete the information you’ve sent us, or to come off our email list.

Children

The site isn’t aimed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has sent us something, let us know and we’ll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.

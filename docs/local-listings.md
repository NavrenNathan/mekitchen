## 4. Getting reviews, the right way

Reviews help a new restaurant more than almost anything else in local search.
They also come with rules: the FTC's rule on consumer reviews (16 CFR
Part 465, in force since October 2024) and Google's review policies.

**Do**
- Ask every customer, the same way. A small QR code at the counter, on the
  receipt or on the bag, linking to Google's "write a review" page (the
  profile's *Ask for reviews* button gives the link).
- Reply to reviews, good and bad, briefly and politely, within a few days.
- Fix what reviews point out, then say so in the reply.

**Don't**
- Write reviews yourself, or have staff, family or friends do it.
- Offer anything for a review: no discounts, free drinks or entries
  (Google bans review incentives outright). The free drink for following on
  Instagram is fine, as long as it never depends on a review.
- Ask only happy customers, or steer unhappy ones somewhere else.
- Buy reviews, followers or ratings, or hide or remove honest negative ones.
- Put testimonials on the website until there are real ones, and then only
  quote them accurately, with permission.

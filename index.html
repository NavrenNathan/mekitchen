Questions

Good to know

Is there anything vegetarian?

Yes. The vegetable broth is built separately rather than thinned from the beef pot, and tofu is available in every category on the menu.

Can I order ahead?

Yes — ordering runs through Square. Pay on your phone, skip the line, grab it off the pickup shelf.

Is the phở gluten-free?

The rice noodles and broth are made without gluten-containing ingredients. It is one shared kitchen, so we cannot promise zero cross-contact.
